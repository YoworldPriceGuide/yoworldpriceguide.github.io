About

Keep track of item prices yourself and see prices reviewed by trusted moderators. Build buy and sell lists, log prices as you find them, and use community data to get a clearer picture of what items are worth.

  • Search the YoWorld item catalogue
  • Track items on your buy & sell lists
  • Log prices manually and view history charts
  • See prices reviewed by trusted moderators

Where does the price data come from?

Prices are calculated using real trade data logged by community members and approved by moderators.

How is the price range worked out?

Once enough logs are approved, the min and max are calculated from actual trades, trimming extreme outliers so one unusually high or low trade doesn't skew the guide.

How accurate is it?

The more price logs an item has, the more accurate its range. Items with few logs may show a wider or less reliable range.

This extension is built using community-made tools and unofficial resources. Neither the item data nor prices shown are official information from BVG.
